# Goal Prompt — Open-Ended Vision

Use this when you want to hand the AI the idea and give it freedom to explore
creatively and technically. Paste into `/goal`.

---

GOAL: Build an AI cooking companion that looks inside your kitchen and helps
you figure out what to make.

The experience I'm chasing: I point my phone at my fridge, my pantry, my
cupboards — photos or video — and an assistant actually *sees* what's there.
When it's unsure, it doesn't guess; it asks, the way a curious friend would:
"What's in the bowl behind the milk? Is that cheddar in the drawer? What are
those leftovers?" I can talk to it out loud and it talks back, or I can type —
my choice, either way. That spoken conversation is the ground floor of the
product, not an add-on. It figures out what I have, then suggests things I could
actually cook right now — with a picture of how the finished dish will look and
a sense of the calories and macros. When I'm short a couple of ingredients, it
helps me get them.

The part that matters most: it learns. Not just for me — for everyone. Every
time a user helps it recognize a messy container, a handwritten label, an
unfamiliar product, that knowledge should make the engine smarter for the whole
community. The system should get better at seeing real kitchens the more people
use it. Treat this shared, collective learning as central to the product, not a
footnote.

Beyond that, I'm not prescribing the how. Choose the tech stack, the
architecture, the models, the data design — whatever you think gives the best
result. Explore. Be creative technically and in the product experience. If you
see a better version of this idea than I've described, chase it.

Constraints are light and mostly about sequence, not method:
- It's a phone-first experience with a real spoken voice assistant — I can talk
  to it and it talks back, hands-free at the fridge, or type if I'd rather. That
  conversation is the heart of the product.
- Grocery ordering is where this is headed (order the missing ingredients and
  cook across the next few days), but it can come after the core loop works.
- Get the "see my kitchen → talk about it → tell me what to cook" loop feeling
  genuinely good before anything else.

Start by telling me how you'd approach it and what the first version should be.
