## The new Enter360 site

Modernize the old Enter360 site into the flagship — it has to *demonstrate* the offering, not just describe it.

Requirements:

- **Portfolio** — full 25-year body of work: SWFA, the Texas projects above, TierLab work (as transcribed), newer AI-era work.
- **Lead system** — none exists today; this is the point of the rebuild.
- **On-site AI agent** — talks to prospects, takes/handles phone calls, sets appointments against the calendar.
- **Back-office agents** — landing page generation, blog posts, SEO.
- **Dogfooding is the sales strategy:** the site sells AI-agent-equipped sites. "I need to be selling sites that include agents" — so the agent on Enter360's own site is the demo.
